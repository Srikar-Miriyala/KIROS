THE KIROS PLATFORM

Engineering a Modular
Carbon Capture Platform.

KIROS integrates solar energy, microalgae cultivation, controlled CO₂ delivery and modular bioreactor systems to explore scalable pathways for biological carbon capture.

Solar Energy Layer

KIROS incorporates solar energy as an integrated part of the platform.

The solar layer is intended to provide renewable electricity that can support system components such as circulation, monitoring and other operating requirements.

The broader design question is how renewable electricity generation can be integrated with biological carbon capture rather than treating the two systems as completely separate infrastructure.

CORE ARCHITECTURE

Modular Bioreactor

KIROS is being developed around modular algae cultivation chambers. The current design direction uses a 330 L cultivation chamber as a modular unit. The idea is to create individual units that can be tested, monitored, maintained, modified, and eventually replicated.

330L Cultivation Volume

Transparent chamber designed for optimal light penetration and algae growth.

Gas Delivery & Mixing

Controlled mechanisms to introduce CO₂ and maintain proper circulation.

Integrated Sensors

Real-time monitoring of pH, temperature, and dissolved oxygen.

CO₂ Integration

CO₂ SOURCE → DELIVERY → DIFFUSION / INJECTION → CULTIVATION

KIROS explores the controlled introduction of CO₂ into microalgae cultivation systems. The engineering challenge is not simply supplying CO₂. The system must consider gas transfer, flow, mixing, cultivation conditions, CO₂ availability and biological response.

KIROS is developing the CO₂ integration mechanism as part of its prototype architecture.

Water Circulation

PUMP → BIOREACTOR → CIRCULATION → RETURN

Water movement is essential to the operation of a controlled algae cultivation system.

KIROS is exploring circulation and mixing mechanisms that can help distribute nutrients, CO₂, light exposure and biological culture through the cultivation volume.

INTELLIGENT CONTROL

Sensor & Monitoring

KIROS is designed around the idea that biological systems should be monitored as engineering systems. The monitoring layer is intended to provide visibility into cultivation and system conditions.

Potential operating parameters being considered: pH, temperature, dissolved oxygen, light, flow, and system status.

Automation

The control layer is intended to connect sensors and system components. Potential control areas include water circulation, CO₂ delivery, pump operation, and environmental conditions. Automation software is part of the development roadmap.

KIROS is developing integrated climate infrastructure combining renewable energy, microalgae cultivation and modular engineering for industrial carbon capture applications.
