THE KIROS PROCESS

From Sunlight and CO₂
to Biomass.

KIROS connects renewable energy, controlled CO₂ delivery and microalgae cultivation through a modular biological system.

01

Solar Energy Capture

Solar energy provides the renewable electricity layer of the KIROS platform. Electricity can support system components such as pumps, monitoring, controls and other operating requirements.

The objective is to integrate renewable energy into the carbon-capture system architecture.

02

CO₂ Injection

Industrial CO₂ is conceptually introduced into the cultivation system through a controlled delivery mechanism.

CO₂ SOURCE → PIPE / DELIVERY → DIFFUSER → CULTIVATION CHAMBER

The prototype work focuses on developing a practical mechanism for introducing CO₂ into the biological system.

03

Algae Photosynthesis

Microalgae use light and CO₂ as part of photosynthetic growth. Within the KIROS cultivation environment, the algae are intended to convert available carbon into biological biomass.

KIROS is investigating cultivation conditions including light, temperature, pH, nutrients, CO₂ availability and circulation, with Chlorella vulgaris as the current research direction.

04

Biomass Harvest

As algae grow, biomass can be separated from the cultivation medium. KIROS includes a harvest pathway in its reactor architecture.

Future research can investigate how harvested biomass may be processed or utilized. (Note: KIROS does not currently claim a finalized commercial biomass product.)

05

System Outputs

The KIROS concept is designed around multiple potential outputs being explored for future viability.

Renewable Electricity

Generated through the solar layer.

Biomass

Produced through microalgae cultivation.

CO₂ Utilization

A targeted biological pathway for utilizing CO₂ within the cultivation system.

Water Reuse

Potential for recirculation within the system, subject to actual process design and validation.

KIROS is developing integrated climate infrastructure combining renewable energy, microalgae cultivation and modular engineering for industrial carbon capture applications.
