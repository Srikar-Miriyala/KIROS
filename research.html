RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Engineering
Through Research.

KIROS combines microalgae research, photovoltaic investigation and bioreactor engineering to explore practical pathways for integrated carbon capture.

style="margin-bottom: 80px; padding: 60px;">

Microalgae Research

KIROS is investigating microalgae as the biological component of its carbon-capture platform.

Current research considerations include species selection, growth conditions, light intensity, temperature, pH, nutrient availability, CO₂ availability and biomass growth.

Chlorella vulgaris is part of the current KIROS research direction. The objective is to understand how cultivation conditions influence system performance before scaling the architecture.

Bioreactor Research

The reactor is being developed as a controlled environment for algae cultivation.

Research and engineering considerations include chamber geometry, light distribution, water circulation, mixing, CO₂ diffusion, temperature, modularity and harvesting.

The current KIROS design direction includes a 330 L modular chamber.

CO₂ Utilization

KIROS is exploring biological pathways for utilizing industrial CO₂ through microalgae cultivation.

Research focuses on the interface between CO₂ source, delivery, gas transfer, cultivation and biomass formation.

Solar Integration

KIROS investigates how photovoltaic energy generation can coexist with biological cultivation.

The engineering challenge includes balancing electricity generation, light availability for algae, physical integration and thermal conditions.

System Monitoring

A biological reactor changes continuously. Monitoring is therefore an important part of the KIROS architecture.

The project is developing an approach around sensing temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, flow, light and system status.

Prototype Validation

The next stage of KIROS development is to move from design toward controlled experimentation.

Planned validation activities include water circulation, sensor integration, closed-loop algae cultivation, and CO₂ delivery to generate real operating data.
