ABOUT KIROS

Building Climate
Infrastructure.

KIROS explores the intersection of renewable energy, biological carbon capture and modular engineering to develop practical pathways for reducing industrial environmental impact.

Why KIROS Exists

Climate change cannot be addressed through renewable energy alone. Industrial emissions require additional approaches to carbon management.

KIROS explores the intersection of solar energy, biological carbon capture, modular engineering and intelligent monitoring.

The goal is to develop systems that can be studied, tested and progressively scaled through engineering.

Our Approach

KIROS follows an engineering-led development path.
Each stage generates information that informs the next.

RESEARCH → DESIGN → PROTOTYPE → VALIDATION → PILOT → SCALE

The First Prototype

The current prototype direction brings together a 330 L modular bioreactor, CO₂ injection mechanism, water circulation, solar integration, sensor architecture, automation and biomass harvesting.
