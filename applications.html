APPLICATIONS

Designed Around
Real-World Emissions.

KIROS is exploring how modular biological carbon-capture systems could integrate with industrial environments where concentrated CO₂ sources and supporting infrastructure are available.

Industrial Facilities

Manufacturing environments can produce continuous or process-related CO₂ emissions.

KIROS could potentially be explored where a suitable CO₂ source, land availability, water resources and supporting infrastructure exist.

Focus: CO₂ source integration, modular deployment, monitoring and system integration.

Thermal Power

Thermal power generation can produce concentrated flue-gas CO₂ streams.

KIROS research can explore whether biological cultivation systems can be integrated downstream of suitable emissions-handling and gas-conditioning systems.

* Note: Biological systems require gas conditioning; raw flue gas cannot be connected directly to algae chambers.

Steel

Steel production involves significant process and energy-related emissions.

KIROS could potentially be explored as part of broader industrial carbon-management infrastructure where suitable CO₂ streams and operating conditions exist.

Cement

Cement manufacturing is a major industrial source of CO₂.

KIROS can explore biological utilization pathways alongside appropriate emissions handling and conditioning infrastructure.

Chemical Industries

Chemical plants often have highly concentrated CO₂ streams suitable for biological integration.

Wastewater

Nutrient-rich industrial wastewater can potentially support microalgae cultivation after appropriate conditioning.

LOOKING FORWARD

Future Infrastructure

The long-term vision for KIROS extends beyond individual industrial sites.

The modular architecture could potentially support distributed climate infrastructure where solar energy, biological systems, CO₂ management, water systems and intelligent monitoring can operate together.

KIROS is developing integrated climate infrastructure combining renewable energy, microalgae cultivation and modular engineering for industrial carbon capture applications.
